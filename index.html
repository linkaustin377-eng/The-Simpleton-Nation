📜 Laws of The Simpleton Nation

  • No 6 7.
  • Saying the words GOONING or GOON or GOONED or GOONER = 1 law break.
  • Saying the N-word = 2 week exile that is Unpardonable
  • No treason.
  • Attempting to bribe the supreme leader will result in a 1 week exile that is Unpardonable
  • No war without the supreme leader’s permission.
  • Everyone votes for supreme leader every other Monday at lunch.
  • Max reign of 4 weeks straight, but you can be re-elected after another person goes for at least 2 weeks straight.
  • No messing with this doc.
  • All laws must get voted on.

⚖️ Law Break & Exile Punishments

  • 3 law breaks = 1 day exile that is Unpardonable
  • 3 law breaks after 1 day exile = 3 day exile that is Unpardonable
  • 3 law breaks after 3 day exile = 1 week exile that is Unpardonable
  • 3 law breaks after 1 week exile = PERMANENT exile that can be reversed if the supreme leader starts a vote where at least ¾ of the members must agree. If the people say no, the vote can only be started after 2 weeks have passed following the previous vote.
  • Law Break Reset: Law break count resets after 1 week of no law-breaking for each person, NOT including exile days.
  • Supreme Leader Exile Power: The supreme leader gets to exile a maximum of 2 people, which does not reset if re-elected, unless the people they exiled are pardoned by them or another supreme leader.
  • 1 count of treason or more = PERMANENT exile that is Unpardonable
  • No changing all the laws above unless revision is necessary.

⚔️ Rules of War (Rival Nations)

If new nations arise, war can be dealt with if the rules below are followed:

  • No physically attacking rival nations’ members, blackmailing them, manipulating them, making them feel unsafe, or making them feel uncomfortable.
  • Must be a competition of some sort decided between the nations with a clear goal.

👑 Rules for Being Supreme Leader

  • You must not discriminate against members based on skin color, country of origin, gender, or religion.
  • You must not show favoritism to members.
  • Must not deny members rights or citizenship because of having a bad history with them.
  • How to Apply: Write a 50 word speech on how you are going to better the nation. Directly speaking your speech isn’t required, but you must present it in some way. Most votes wins.
  • Candidates cannot self-vote.
